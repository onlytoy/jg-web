设计特点

Design Highlights

设计方向 中文 English
日式极简 大面积留白与克制装饰，建立稳定可信的日本 B2B 气质。主视觉上边距 141.62px，各区块纵向留白 86–116px。 Generous white space and restrained decoration create a credible Japanese B2B tone, with a 141.62px hero inset and 86–116px section rhythm.
品牌色系统 以 #0a1324 承担文本与页脚基色，#02387e 与 #153b6c 分担主按钮与产品卡，#2847fc 仅用于章节编号点睛。 #0a1324 anchors text and the footer, #02387e and #153b6c carry actions and product cards, and #2847fc appears only as a section-number accent.
双字体系统 正文用 Zen Kaku Gothic New，数字用 Poppins。以 unicode-range 仅将 0–9 映射到 Poppins，日文句子内的数字自动切换字体，无需额外标记。 Zen Kaku Gothic New for text, Poppins for numerals. A unicode-range subset maps only 0–9 to Poppins, so digits inside Japanese sentences switch automatically with no markup.
光学对齐 Logo、标题、正文与按钮共用同一基准轴，并补偿假名字形边距与图片透明留白，做到视觉边缘（而非盒子边缘）严格对齐。 Logo, headline, copy, and button share one alignment axis, with kana side bearings and image padding compensated so the ink edges line up — not just the boxes.
模块化卡片 缩略图与主卡构成「索引—详情」关系，统一圆角语言便于快速扫描，页码 1/4 提供进度反馈。 Thumbnails index into a detail card with a shared rounded-card language for fast scanning, and a 1/4 counter gives progress feedback.
圆角与投影 按钮采用 40px 全圆角，转化面板 20px 圆角配 0 20px 40px 低透明度投影，提升现代感而不过度装饰。 40px pill buttons and a 20px-radius conversion panel with a low-opacity 0 20px 40px shadow modernise the look without over-decorating.
滚动叙事 产品生态区采用原生 sticky 固定推进四屏，而非劫持滚轮事件，完整保留触控板惯性与滚动条拖拽体验。 The ecosystem block advances four slides using native sticky pinning rather than hijacking wheel events, preserving trackpad inertia and scrollbar dragging.
动效语言 全站共用 easeOutQuint 缓动与 240 / 420 / 640ms 三档时长；图片交叉淡入伴随轻微缩放，文案按 90 / 150 / 210ms 阶梯入场。 One motion language: easeOutQuint easing across 240/420/640ms tiers, images cross-fading with a slight scale, and copy staggering in at 90/150/210ms.
响应式体验 四档断点（1660 / 1440 / 1200 / 900px）。绝对定位的复合区块在窄屏转为网格与横向滚动；画面高度不足时自动放弃固定滚动。 Four breakpoints (1660/1440/1200/900px). Absolutely composed blocks reflow to grid and horizontal scroll on narrow screens, and pinning is dropped automatically when the viewport is too short.
性能优化 主视觉图输出多档 WebP 并以 srcset 按需取用（135KB 即可在 Retina 达到 1:1），数字字体按字重子集化至每档 1.1KB。 The hero ships as multi-step WebP served through srcset (135KB achieves 1:1 on Retina), and the numeral font is subset to 1.1KB per weight.
无障碍 轮播具备 tablist 语义与键盘方向键操作，并在 prefers-reduced-motion 下降级为无位移的即时切换。 The carousel exposes tablist semantics with arrow-key control and degrades to instant, motion-free switching under prefers-reduced-motion.
信息流 遵循「品牌定位 → 规模数据 → 产品生态 → 转化」的叙事顺序。 The narrative follows Brand → Scale → Solutions → Conversion.
